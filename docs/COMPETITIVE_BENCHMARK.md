# Competitive Benchmark — Fooladban
Updated: 2026-11-26

## Scope and method
This is a public-product capability benchmark, not a claim that every vendor or feature in the market has been exhaustively audited. We reviewed public product pages, app listings and published feature descriptions. Vendor marketing claims are not treated as independently verified accuracy evidence. Competitors are grouped by job-to-be-done: benchmark data, regional pricing, trading/marketplaces, analytics and mobile price access.

| Product | Publicly documented strengths | Design lessons for Fooladban |

## Strategic conclusion
Fooladban should not try to outspend global price-reporting agencies on proprietary analyst assessments. Its defensible wedge is a user-controlled monitoring layer for fragmented public/private sources, with unusually strong Iran + MENA product normalization, source freshness/quality, alerts and decision-support analytics.

The product must clearly distinguish:
1. A source-reported quote (raw observation).
2. A normalized comparable price (same currency, unit, VAT, delivery basis, grade and product spec).
3. A third-party benchmark (licensed/attributed).
4. A model estimate or forecast (never presented as an observed quote).
